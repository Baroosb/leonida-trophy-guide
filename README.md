**Leonida Platinum Atlas** is an offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5. It covers all 55 official trophies — every name, description and grade shown exactly as PlayStation publishes it — with an original, source-cited guide for each one: PowerPyx, The Games Wiki, KeenGamer and TechForNerd are credited beside the trophies their reporting informs, and the official list is cited where a guide rests on it alone. A five-phase platinum roadmap (story first, then the 100% checklist, then the stranger strands) orders the run, and the game's one potentially missable trophy — Positive Mental Attitude, with its Chapter 1 balcony meditation — is flagged in the list, in its detail view and at the top of the roadmap. The tracker saves check-offs in localStorage with a progress bar and per-grade counts, exports and imports progress as a JSON file, filters by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online — the game has none — and misc), and offers a spoiler shield that hides story trophies' names and descriptions until they are earned. Everything is static files: open the app offline and it reaches for nothing.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output node main.ts
node serve.ts
```
